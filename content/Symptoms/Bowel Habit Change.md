# 💩 Bowel Habit Change (อุจจาระผิดปกติ)

> "A persistent change in bowel habits is a key presentation of colorectal malignancy. The diagnostic path must separate benign functional disorders from organic disease."

---

## 🎯 30-Second Rounding Guide
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Immediate Ward Approach:**
> 1. **Rule Out Colorectal Cancer (CRC)**: Any new-onset change in bowel habits in a patient **$>50$ years** (or $>40$ years with red flags) is CRC until proven otherwise. **Schedule Colonoscopy.**
> 2. **Identify Organic vs. Functional**:
>    - *Organic*: Associated with weight loss, nocturnal symptoms, anemia, blood in stool, or elevated inflammatory markers.
>    - *Functional* (e.g., IBS): Symptoms relate to stress, fluctuate without weight loss/anemia, and do not wake the patient from sleep.
> 3. **Perform Digital Rectal Examination (DRE)** on all patients to evaluate for rectal masses, hemorrhoids, tone, and occult blood.

---

## 🔍 Definition & Physiology

A bowel habit change represents a significant, persistent deviation (typically $>4-6$ weeks) from a patient's baseline stool frequency, consistency, or ease of passage.
* **Colonic Transit Time**: Governed by autonomic innervation. Slow transit $\rightarrow$ constipation; rapid transit or impaired mucosal absorption $\rightarrow$ diarrhea.
* **Pencil-Thin Stools**: Indicates a mechanical narrowing of the distal colon lumen, classically associated with left-sided descending or sigmoid colorectal carcinoma.

---

## 🗣️ Key History Taking

* **Character of Change**:
  * *New-onset progressive constipation + "pencil-thin" stools* $\rightarrow$ Left-sided colorectal carcinoma.
  * *Alternating diarrhea and constipation* $\rightarrow$ Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS-M) or colon cancer.
  * *Chronic diarrhea with blood and mucus, abdominal pain* $\rightarrow$ Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD: Crohn's/UC).
* **Associated Symptoms**:
  * Involuntary weight loss, night sweats, anorexia $\rightarrow$ Cancer or TB.
  * Orthostatic dizziness, fatigue $\rightarrow$ Iron Deficiency Anemia (chronic blood loss).
  * Nocturnal diarrhea (waking up at night to defecate) $\rightarrow$ **Organic cause** (never functional).
* **Medication History**: Opioids, calcium channel blockers (constipation), NSAIDs, antibiotics (diarrhea), iron supplements.

---

## 🩺 Physical Examination

* **Vital Signs**: Document BP/HR (tachycardia in anemia/dehydration) and Temp.
* **Abdomen**:
  * *Distension* $\rightarrow$ Impending obstruction or fecal impaction.
  * *Palpable masses*: Check the RLQ (cecal cancer, Crohn's disease) and LLQ (diverticulitis, sigmoid cancer).
* **Digital Rectal Examination (DRE) (Mandatory)**:
  * Check for rectal masses or strictures.
  * Evaluate sphincter tone and look for fecal impaction.
  * Inspect glove for **hematochezia**, **melena**, or perform a fecal occult blood test.
* **Extra-intestinal signs**: Pallor (anemia), skin tags/fistulas (Crohn's disease), thyroid enlargement (hypo/hyperthyroidism).

## 📂 Differential Diagnosis

| Origin | Common / Functional | Dangerous / Organic (Can't Miss) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Neoplastic** | None | • **Colorectal Cancer (CRC)**<br>• Large adenomatous polyps |
| **Inflammatory** | • Post-infectious IBS | • **Ulcerative Colitis** / **Crohn's Disease** (IBD)<br>• Chronic Diverticulitis |
| **Functional** | • Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS-D, IBS-C, IBS-M) | None |
| **Endocrine / Metabolic**| • Medication-induced (diuretics, CCBs, opioids) | • **[[Hyperthyroidism]]** (causes diarrhea)<br>• **[[Hypothyroidism]]** (causes constipation)<br>• Uremic enteropathy |
| **Infectious** | • Chronic Giardiasis / Lactose intolerance | • **Tuberculous Enteritis** (common in endemic regions) |

## 🚨 Red Flags

> [!CAUTION]
> **Refer immediately for Colonoscopy if any red flags are present:**
> * Age **$>50$ years** with new-onset, unexplained change in bowel habits.
> * Rectal bleeding (hematochezia) or positive fecal occult blood.
> * Unexplained Iron Deficiency Anemia (low ferritin).
> * Involuntary weight loss ($>5\%$ in 6 months) or persistent fever.
> * **Nocturnal diarrhea** (wakes patient from sleep).
> * Family history of Colorectal Cancer or IBD in a first-degree relative.
> * Palpable abdominal or rectal mass.

---

## 🧪 Investigations

* **First-line Screen**:
  * **[[CBC]]**: Screen for microcytic anemia (suggestive of chronic GI blood loss).
  * **Fecal Occult Blood Test (FOBT) / Fecal Immunochemical Test (FIT)**: FIT is preferred (specific for human globin, does not require dietary restriction).
  * **Thyroid Panel (TSH / Free T4)**: Rule out hypo/hyperthyroidism.
  * **Renal and Liver function, Electrolytes**.
  * **Inflammatory Markers**: ESR, CRP, or Fecal Calprotectin (highly sensitive for IBD, normal in IBS).
* **Diagnostic Gold Standard**:
  * **Colonoscopy with Biopsy**: Diagnostic and therapeutic (polypectomy). Indicated for any patient with red flags or new change in bowel habits $>50$ years.
  * **CT Abdomen & Pelvis**: Useful to evaluate extraluminal extension of colon cancer, diverticulitis, or intra-abdominal masses.

---

## 🏥 Management Principles

1. **Colorectal Cancer**:
   - Staging (CT scan, CEA marker) followed by surgical resection +/- adjuvant chemotherapy/radiation.
2. **Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD)**:
   - Induction and maintenance therapy with 5-ASAs (Mesalazine), systemic corticosteroids (for flares), immunomodulators (Azathioprine), or biological agents (anti-TNF).
3. **Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)**:
   - Dietary changes: High-fiber diet for constipation; Low-FODMAP diet for bloating/diarrhea.
   - Symptomatic relief: Loperamide for IBS-D; osmotic laxatives (PEG) for IBS-C; antispasmodics (Mebeverine) for pain.

---

## 💡 Clinical Pearls

> [!TIP]
> * **Right-Sided vs. Left-Sided Colon Cancer**:
>   - *Right-sided (Ascending colon)*: Larger lumen, liquid stool. Cancer here rarely causes obstruction. Patients present with **chronic microcytic anemia (occult blood loss), fatigue, and weight loss**.
>   - *Left-sided (Descending/Sigmoid colon)*: Smaller lumen, solid stool. Cancer here presents with **obstructive symptoms, changing bowel habits (constipation/diarrhea), and bright red blood coating the stool**.
> * **Nocturnal Diarrhea Rule**: Irritable Bowel Syndrome is a functional disorder of gut motility and does not occur during sleep. Diarrhea that wakes a patient from a sound sleep is a definitive sign of **organic pathology** (e.g., IBD, secretory tumor, microscopic colitis).
> * **Iron Deficiency Anemia in Men**: Any adult male or postmenopausal female with unexplained iron deficiency anemia has a gastrointestinal malignancy (specifically colon cancer) until proven otherwise. Full upper and lower GI scopes are mandatory.
