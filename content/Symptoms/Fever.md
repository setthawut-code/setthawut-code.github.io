# 🌡️ Fever (ไข้)

> "Fever is a manifestation of disease, not a diagnosis."

---

## 🎯 30-Second Rounding Guide
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **5 Questions to answer immediately for every fever patient on ward rounds:**
> 1. **Is it true fever?** Core temp $\ge 38^\circ\text{C}$ vs. Hyperthermia.
> 2. **Infectious vs. Non-infectious?** Check history, exposures, labs.
> 3. **What is the source?** Head-to-toe systematic search.
> 4. **Is there [[Sepsis]]?** Check vital signs, qSOFA/SOFA, perfusion.
> 5. **Empiric Antibiotics needed?** Neutropenic fever, septic shock, meningitis $\rightarrow$ **Give immediately!**

---

## 🔍 Definition & Pathophysiology

| Condition | Hypothalamic Set-Point | Pathophysiology | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fever (Pyrexia)** | **Elevated** (by pyrogens: IL-1, IL-6, TNF-$\alpha$, PGE2) | Physiological response to infection/inflammation | Infection, Autoimmune, Malignancy |
| **Hyperthermia** | **Normal** | Heat production exceeds heat dissipation | Heat stroke, NMS, Serotonin syndrome, Malignant hyperthermia |

## 📂 Clinical Framework (DDx)

```text
Fever
├── Infectious
│   ├── Respiratory
│   ├── Urinary Tract (UTI)
│   ├── Hepatobiliary
│   └── CNS
└── Non-Infectious
    ├── Autoimmune / Inflammatory
    ├── Malignancy
    ├── Drug Fever
    └── Endocrine / Miscellaneous
```

### 1. Infectious Causes (Most Common)
* **Respiratory**: [[Community Acquired Pneumonia]] (CAP), [[Tuberculosis]] (TB), [[Lung Abscess]]
* **Urinary Tract**: [[Acute Cystitis]], [[Acute Pyelonephritis]]
* **Hepatobiliary**: [[Acute Cholangitis]], [[Acute Cholecystitis]], [[Liver Abscess]]
* **CNS**: [[Meningitis]], [[Brain Abscess]]
* **Cardiovascular**: [[Infective Endocarditis]]
* **Gastrointestinal**: [[Acute Gastroenteritis]], [[Typhoid Fever]]
* **Tropical / Endemic**: [[Dengue]], [[Malaria]], [[Leptospirosis]], [[Melioidosis]]

### 2. Autoimmune & Inflammatory
* **Connective Tissue Disease**: [[Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]] (SLE), [[Adult Onset Still Disease]] (AOSD), [[Rheumatoid Arthritis]] (RA)
* **Vasculitis**: [[Giant Cell Arteritis]], [[ANCA Vasculitis]]

### 3. Malignancy
* **Hematologic**: [[Lymphoma]] (B symptoms), [[Leukemia]]
* **Solid Tumors**: [[Renal Cell Carcinoma]] (RCC), [[Hepatocellular Carcinoma]] (HCC)

### 4. Miscellaneous & Endocrine
* **Drug Fever**: Commonly $\beta$-lactams, sulfonamides, phenytoin, allopurinol.
* **Endocrine**: [[Thyrotoxicosis]] (Thyroid storm), [[Adrenal Crisis]]
* **Thromboembolic**: [[Deep Vein Thrombosis]] (DVT), [[Pulmonary Embolism]] (PE)

## 📈 Fever Patterns

* **Continuous Fever** (Fluctuation $< 1^\circ\text{C}$, never normal): Typhoid fever, lobar pneumonia.
* **Intermittent Fever** (Returns to normal daily): Malaria, pyogenic abscess.
* **Remittent Fever** (Fluctuation $> 1^\circ\text{C}$, never normal): [[Infective Endocarditis]].
* **Pel-Ebstein Fever** (Cyclical: days of fever followed by days of afebrile period): [[Hodgkin Lymphoma]] *(rare in practice)*.

## 🗣️ Key History Taking

### ⏳ Characterize the Fever
* **Acute** ($< 2$ weeks) vs. **Subacute** ($2-4$ weeks) vs. **Chronic** ($> 4$ weeks)

### 🧩 Syndromic Associated Symptoms
> [!TIP]
> **Use the "Fever + X" approach to narrow the source:**

| Associated Symptom | Suspected Diagnosis |
| :--- | :--- |
| **+ Cough & Sputum** | [[Community Acquired Pneumonia]] (CAP), [[Tuberculosis]], [[Lung Abscess]] |
| **+ Dysuria & Flank pain** | UTI, [[Acute Pyelonephritis]] |
| **+ Jaundice & RUQ pain** | [[Acute Cholangitis]] (Charcot's Triad), [[Viral Hepatitis]], [[Leptospirosis]] |
| **+ Weight loss & Night sweats** | [[Tuberculosis]], HIV, [[Lymphoma]], Malignancy |
| **+ Back pain** | Vertebral osteomyelitis, Spinal epidural abscess |
| **+ New Heart Murmur** | [[Infective Endocarditis]] |
| **+ Headache & Stiff neck** | [[Meningitis]] |

### 🌍 Crucial Exposures
* **Travel**: Forests/borders ([[Malaria]], [[Dengue]]), agricultural areas/floods ([[Leptospirosis]], scrub typhus).
* **Occupation**: Farmers/livestock handlers ([[Brucellosis]], [[Leptospirosis]], [[Melioidosis]]).
* **Animals**: Dogs/cats (rabies, pasteurella), rodents ([[Leptospirosis]], Hantavirus), birds (psittacosis).
* **Sexual Risk**: Acute HIV conversion, secondary syphilis, viral hepatitis.
* **Host Factors**: Immunocompromised status (HIV, chemo, steroids, post-transplant).

## 🩺 Physical Examination

* **General**: Look for **Toxic appearance** (signs of sepsis, meningitis).
* **Vital Signs**: Document BP, HR, RR, Temp, SpO2. Look for relative bradycardia (Fever + normal/low HR, e.g., Typhoid, Leptospirosis, atypical pneumonia).
* **Skin**:
  * *Maculopapular rash*: Viral exanthems, drug eruption.
  * *Petechiae/Purpura*: [[Dengue]], meningococcemia, DIC.
  * *Eschar*: Scrub typhus.
  * *Splinter hemorrhages / Janeway lesions / Osler nodes*: [[Infective Endocarditis]].
* **HEENT**: Meningeal signs (neck stiffness, Kernig's/Brudzinski's), lymphadenopathy ([[Tuberculosis]], EBV/mononucleosis, [[Lymphoma]], HIV).
* **Cardiovascular**: New murmur ([[Infective Endocarditis]]).
* **Chest**: Crackles/consolidation signs (pneumonia).
* **Abdomen**: Hepatosplenomegaly (malaria, enteric fever, lymphoma/leukemia), RUQ tenderness (cholangitis, liver abscess).
* **Musculoskeletal**: Joint effusion (septic arthritis), spinal tenderness (discitis/osteomyelitis).

## 🧪 Investigations

### 1. Basic Workup
* **[[CBC]]**: Look for leukocytosis with left shift (bacterial), leukopenia/thrombocytopenia ([[Dengue]], [[Malaria]], [[Sepsis]], SLE).
* **Renal & Electrolytes**: Creatinine (AKI in sepsis/leptospirosis), hyponatremia (atypical pneumonia, adrenal insufficiency).
* **Liver Function Tests**: Elevated AST/ALT/Alkaline phosphatase (hepatobiliary source, leptospirosis, viral hepatitis).
* **[[Urinalysis]]**: Pyuria, hematuria, nitrites (UTI/pyelonephritis).
* **[[Chest X-Ray]]**: Routine first-line test to rule out pulmonary consolidation, effusion, or miliary TB.

### 2. Microbiology & Special Tests
* **Blood Culture $\times$ 2 sets**: *Obtain BEFORE administering antibiotics.* Essential for suspected sepsis, IE, or unexplained fever.
* **Urine Culture**: If UA is abnormal or patient is elderly/catheterized.
* **Sputum Gram stain & Culture, AFB smear**: If respiratory symptoms.
* **Serology/PCR**: Dengue NS1/IgM, Leptospira Ab, Malaria thick/thin smear.

## 🚨 Red Flags & Sepsis Identification

> [!CAUTION]
> **If any of these are present, initiate emergency management:**
> * **Sepsis (qSOFA $\ge 2$)**:
>   * SBP $\le 100$ mmHg
>   * RR $\ge 22$ breaths/min
>   * Altered mental status (GCS < 15)
> * **Septic Shock**: Hypotension requiring vasopressors to maintain MAP $\ge 65$ mmHg AND Serum Lactate $> 2$ mmol/L despite fluid resuscitation.
> * **Meningitis signs**: Altered consciousness, neck stiffness, seizures.
> * **Acute Cholangitis**: Charcot's triad (Fever + Jaundice + RUQ pain).

## 🏥 Clinical & Exam Pearls

### 👴 Fever in the Elderly
- May present **without** fever due to impaired thermoregulation.
- Common presentations of infection in elderly: **Acute delirium (altered mental status), falls, functional decline, or tachypnea.**

### 📉 Neutropenic Fever
- Defined as a single temp $> 38.3^\circ\text{C}$ (or $> 38.0^\circ\text{C}$ for $>1$ hour) + Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) $< 500/\mu\text{L}$.
- **Medical Emergency!** Draw blood cultures and start empiric broad-spectrum anti-pseudomonal $\beta$-lactam (e.g., Piperacillin/Tazobactam, Cefepime, or Meropenem) within **60 minutes** of arrival. Do not wait for results.

### 📚 Quick Diagnostic Matches
* **Fever + New Murmur** $\rightarrow$ [[Infective Endocarditis]] until proven otherwise.
* **Fever + Hypotension** $\rightarrow$ [[Sepsis]] / [[Septic Shock]] until proven otherwise.
* **Fever + Jaundice + RUQ Pain** $\rightarrow$ [[Acute Cholangitis]] until proven otherwise.
* **Fever + Neck Stiffness** $\rightarrow$ [[Meningitis]] until proven otherwise.
* **Fever + Weight Loss + Night Sweats** $\rightarrow$ [[Tuberculosis]] or [[Lymphoma]] until proven otherwise.
