# 🦠 HIV Infection and AIDS (การติดเชื้อเอชไอวีและโรคเอดส์)

> **Definition**: Chronic infection caused by Human Immunodeficiency Virus type 1 or 2 (HIV-1, HIV-2), leading to progressive depletion of CD4+ T lymphocytes, profound cellular immunosuppression, and Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome (AIDS).

---

## 🦠 Pathophysiology & Viral Replication

* **Structure**: Enveloped RNA retrovirus.
  * **gp120**: Surface glycoprotein, binds to **CD4 receptor** and chemokine co-receptors (**CCR5** on macrophages early/macrophage-tropic; **CXCR4** on T cells late/T-cell-tropic).
  * **gp41**: Transmembrane glycoprotein, mediates fusion and host cell entry.
  * *Note: Homozygous mutation in CCR5 gene (CCR5-delta32) confers resistance to HIV-1 infection.*
* **Replication Cycle**:
  1. **Binding & Fusion**: gp120/gp41 binding $\rightarrow$ fusion $\rightarrow$ viral capsid enters cell.
  2. **Reverse Transcription**: Viral RNA transcribed to double-stranded DNA by **Reverse Transcriptase**. *Error-prone enzyme; causes rapid mutation.*
  3. **Integration**: Viral DNA integrated into host genome by **Integrase**.
  4. **Transcription & Translation**: Host RNA polymerase transcribes viral DNA.
  5. **Assembly & Budding**: New virions assemble at cell membrane.
  6. **Maturation**: **Protease** cleaves polyproteins into functional viral proteins to form mature infectious virus.

## 🔍 Clinical Stages

### 1. Acute HIV Infection (Acute Retroviral Syndrome)
* Occurs $2-4$ weeks post-exposure.
* **Symptoms**: Mononucleosis-like syndrome (fever, sore throat, generalized painful lymphadenopathy, maculopapular rash, myalgias, arthralgias, diarrhea, aseptic meningitis).
* **Lab Profile**: High viral load (extremely infectious), high p24 antigen, but **HIV antibody tests may be negative/indeterminate** (window period).

### 2. Chronic HIV Infection (Clinical Latency)
* Asymptomatic or persistent generalized lymphadenopathy (PGL).
* Lasts average $8-10$ years. CD4 count slowly declines ($50-100$ cells/$\mu\text{L}$ per year) due to continuous viral replication in lymphoid organs.

### 3. AIDS (Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome)
* Defined by:
  1. **CD4+ T-cell count $< 200$ cells/$\mu\text{L}$** OR
  2. **Presence of an AIDS-defining condition**, regardless of CD4 count (e.g., *Pneumocystis jirovecii* pneumonia, Esophageal Candidiasis, Cryptococcal Meningitis, Cerebral Toxoplasmosis, Kaposi Sarcoma, CMV Retinitis, disseminated MAC).

## 🔬 Investigations

* **Diagnostic Algorithm**:
  * **Screening**: **4th Generation Antigen/Antibody Combination Assay**. Detects **HIV-1/2 antibodies** and **p24 antigen**. (p24 appears early, reducing window period to $\sim 14$ days).
  * **Confirmation**: If screening positive, perform **HIV-1/HIV-2 Antibody Differentiation Immunoassay**.
  * **Discrepant/Indeterminate**: Run **HIV-1 RNA RT-PCR** (Viral Load).
* **Monitoring Parameters**:
  * **CD4+ T-cell Count**: Measures immune status, guides initiation of prophylaxis for opportunistic infections.
  * **HIV-1 RNA Viral Load (PCR)**: Standard for monitoring treatment efficacy and adherence. Target is **Undetectable** ($<20-50$ copies/mL).
* **Pre-treatment Screening**:
  * **HLA-B\*5701 Genotyping**: **Mandatory** before initiating **Abacavir** (detects risk for life-threatening hypersensitivity reaction).
  * HBsAg/HBV DNA (co-infection affects ART choices; Tenofovir covers both).
  * Baseline LFTs, renal function, lipid profile.

## 🏥 Management

### 1. Antiretroviral Therapy (ART)
* **Indication**: Recommend ART for **all** HIV-infected individuals immediately upon diagnosis, regardless of CD4+ count (increases survival, prevents transmission: **U=U [Undetectable = Untransmittable]**).
* **Standard Initial Regimen**:
  * **2 NRTIs** (Nucleoside Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitors) + **1 INSTI** (Integrase Strand Transfer Inhibitor).
  * **First-line Regimen**: **TDF/FTC/DTG** (Tenofovir disoproxil fumarate + Emtricitabine + Dolutegravir) OR **TAF/FTC/DTG** (Tenofovir alafenamide + Emtricitabine + Dolutegravir) as a single daily tablet.
  * *Alternative*: **Abacavir/3TC/DTG** (only if HLA-B\*5701 negative).

### 2. Antiretroviral Drug Side Effects

| Class | Key Drugs | Main Side Effects & Contraindications |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NRTIs** | **Tenofovir (TDF)** | • **Renal toxicity** (Fanconi syndrome/proximal tubulopathy)<br>• **Osteomalacia** / bone density loss<br>*Note: TAF has significantly less renal and bone toxicity than TDF.* |
| | **Abacavir (ABC)** | • **Hypersensitivity reaction** (fever, rash, GI symptoms, dyspnea). **Contraindicated in HLA-B\*5701 positive.** |
| | **Zidovudine (AZT)** | • **Megaloblastic anemia** / bone marrow suppression. |
| **NNRTIs** | **Efavirenz (EFV)** | • **Neuropsychiatric effects** (vivid dreams, insomnia, depression, psychosis, suicidality). |
| **INSTIs** | **Dolutegravir (DTG)** | • Insomnia, weight gain, benign creatinine elevation (inhibits tubular secretion of creatinine without altering GFR). |
| **PIs** | **Atazanavir (ATV)**<br>**Darunavir (DRV)** | • **Metabolic syndrome** (hyperlipidemia, insulin resistance, lipodystrophy)<br>• Hyperbilirubinemia (Atazanavir inhibits UGT1A1; causes scleral icterus)<br>• **CYP3A4 inhibitors** (many drug-drug interactions). |

### 3. Prevention (PrEP & PEP)
* **Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP)**: Daily oral **TDF/FTC** (or TAF/FTC) for high-risk uninfected individuals.
* **Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP)**: 
  * Initiate within **72 hours** of exposure (occupational or sexual).
  * Regimen: **TDF/FTC** (or TAF/FTC) + **Dolutegravir** (or Raltegravir) for **28 days**.

---

## 💡 Clinical Pearls

* > [!IMPORTANT]
  > **U=U (Undetectable = Untransmittable)**: A patient on ART with a confirmed undetectable viral load for $\ge 6$ months has zero risk of transmitting HIV to sexual partners.
* > [!WARNING]
  > **Abacavir Hypersensitivity**: If a patient started on Abacavir develops fever, rash, and respiratory/GI symptoms, **stop Abacavir immediately and permanently**. Re-challenge can lead to fatal anaphylactic shock.
* > [!TIP]
  > **Hepatitis B Co-infection**: Tenofovir (TDF or TAF) + Emtricitabine/Lamivudine treat both HIV and HBV. Discontinuing these drugs in co-infected patients can cause a **severe, life-threatening flare of Hepatitis B**.
