# 🦠 Zika Virus Infection (การติดเชื้อไวรัสซิกา)

> **Definition**: Mosquito-borne viral infection caused by the Zika virus (genus *Flavivirus*, family *Flaviviridae*), presenting as a mild, self-limiting febrile illness but carrying severe teratogenic risks (**Congenital Zika Syndrome**) and neurological complications.

---

## 🦠 Transmission & Pathophysiology

* **Transmission Routes**:
  * **Vector**: Bite of infected ***Aedes aegypti*** (and *Aedes albopictus*) mosquitoes (day-biting).
  * **Vertical**: Mother-to-fetus (transplacental transmission).
  * **Sexual**: Venereal transmission via semen (virus remains viable in semen for months).
  * Blood transfusion, laboratory exposure.
* **Pathogenesis**:
  * **Adults**: Virus infects dermal fibroblasts and dendritic cells $\rightarrow$ lymph nodes $\rightarrow$ blood. Causes mild vasculitis. Associated with post-infectious autoimmune response leading to **Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS)**.
  * **Fetus**: Virus crosses the placenta and selectively infects **neural progenitor cells (radial glial cells)** in the fetal brain. Inhibits cell division, triggers apoptosis, and disrupts cortical development, resulting in microcephaly and severe brain damage.

---

## 🔍 Clinical Manifestations

### 1. Acute Zika Fever ($20\%$ of cases; $80\%$ asymptomatic)
* Typically mild, lasting $2-7$ days.
* **Symptoms**:
  * Low-grade fever.
  * **Maculopapular rash** (often intensely pruritic, starting on face and spreading to body).
  * **Non-purulent conjunctivitis** (bilateral red eyes without discharge).
  * **Arthralgia** (pain in small joints of hands and feet) or myalgia.
  * Retro-orbital pain, headache.

### 2. Congenital Zika Syndrome (CZS)
Severe pattern of birth defects in infants infected *in utero*:
* **Severe Microcephaly** with partially collapsed skull.
* Thin cerebral cortex with subcortical **intracranial calcifications**.
* Chorioretinal atrophy, focal pigmentary retinal mottling.
* **Arthrogryposis** (congenital joint contractures in multiple limbs).
* Hypertonia, spasticity, and severe cognitive/motor impairment.

### 3. Neurological Complications
* **Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS)**: Strongly associated with Zika virus infection. Presents as acute, ascending flaccid paralysis with albuminocytologic dissociation in CSF.

---

## 🔬 Investigations

* **RT-PCR (Preferred/Gold Standard)**:
  * Detects Zika virus RNA in **serum** (detectable up to $7$ days post-onset) or **urine** (detectable up to $14$ days post-onset; **urine has higher viral load and longer shedding period**).
* **Serology**:
  * **Zika IgM ELISA**: Positive after day 4 of symptoms.
  * > [!WARNING]
    > **Cross-Reactivity**: High cross-reactivity between Zika IgM and other flaviviruses (Dengue, Japanese Encephalitis, Yellow Fever). A positive Zika IgM must be confirmed with the **Plaque Reduction Neutralization Test (PRNT)** to establish definitive diagnosis.

---

## 🏥 Management & Counseling

No specific antiviral therapy. Management is purely supportive:

### 1. Supportive Care
* Rest, aggressive oral hydration, and antipyretics (Acetaminophen).
* > [!IMPORTANT]
  > **Avoid NSAIDs/Aspirin**: Do not administer NSAIDs or Aspirin until [[Dengue Infection]] has been ruled out, to prevent worsening bleeding complications.

### 2. Sexual Transmission Prevention & Conception Counseling
* **Men** returning from Zika-endemic areas: Use condoms or abstain from sex for **at least 3 months** (regardless of whether they had symptoms), as the virus persists in semen.
* **Women** returning from Zika-endemic areas: Wait **at least 2 months** before attempting pregnancy.
* **Pregnant women**: Partner returning from endemic areas must use condoms or abstain from intercourse for the entire duration of the pregnancy.

---

## 💡 Clinical Pearls

* > [!IMPORTANT]
  > **Dengue vs. Zika vs. Chikungunya**:
  > * **Dengue**: High fever, severe myalgia/bone pain, **severe thrombocytopenia, plasma leakage/shock**.
  > * **Zika**: Low fever, **intense pruritic rash, conjunctivitis (red eyes)**, microcephaly in fetus, GBS association.
  > * **Chikungunya**: High fever, **debilitating, prolonged polyarthralgia/arthritis** (often chronic, symmetrical, affecting hands/wrists/ankles), no microcephaly.
* > [!WARNING]
  > **Asymptomatic Transmission**: Men can transmit Zika sexually to partners even if they never developed any symptoms of Zika fever. Adhere strictly to the 3-month condom rule.
