# 🦠 Influenza (โรคไข้หวัดใหญ่)

> **Definition**: An acute, highly contagious viral respiratory tract infection caused by Influenza A or B viruses, presenting with abrupt onset of systemic and respiratory symptoms.

---

## 🦠 Pathophysiology & Genetic Variation
* **Structure**: Enveloped RNA virus with surface glycoproteins:
  * **Hemagglutinin (HA)**: Binds sialic acid receptors on host respiratory epithelial cells to enable viral entry.
  * **Neuraminidase (NA)**: Cleaves sialic acid to release newly formed viral particles from infected host cells.
* **Genetic Mechanisms of Variation**:
  * **Antigenic Drift**: Gradual accumulation of minor **point mutations** in HA or NA genes. Occurs in both **Influenza A and B**. Responsible for seasonal epidemics (requires annual vaccine reformulation).
  * **Antigenic Shift**: Sudden, major **reassortment** of viral genomic segments between different strains (e.g., human + avian + swine) co-infecting a single host. **Occurs only in Influenza A** (which has animal reservoirs). Responsible for global pandemics.

---

## 🔍 Clinical Manifestations
* **Classic Presentation**: Abrupt onset (often within hours) of:
  * **Systemic**: High **[[Fever]]**, chills, severe **[[Myalgia]]** (classically back and legs), headache, and profound fatigue.
  * **Respiratory**: Dry cough, sore throat, and nasal congestion.
* **Complications**:
  * **Primary Influenza Pneumonia**: Severe, rapidly progressive viral lung invasion causing ARDS.
  * **Secondary Bacterial Pneumonia**: Post-viral bacterial invasion. Classically presents as initial clinical improvement followed by a **recrudescence of fever and cough** with purulent sputum. Common pathogens: ***Streptococcus pneumoniae***, ***Staphylococcus aureus*** (extremely severe/necrotizing; classic post-influenza), and *H. influenzae*.
  * Myocarditis, myositis (elevated CK), Guillain-Barré syndrome, and Reye syndrome (in children taking aspirin).

---

## 🔬 Investigations
* **RT-PCR (Gold Standard)**: Highest sensitivity and specificity; differentiates between Influenza A and B and identifies specific subtypes.
* **Rapid Influenza Diagnostic Test (RIDT)**: Enzyme immunoassay. Results in $<30$ minutes. Good specificity ($>90\%$), but moderate sensitivity ($50-70\%$). A negative RIDT does not rule out influenza.
* **[[Chest X-Ray]]**: Indicated if pneumonia is suspected. Shows bilateral interstitial infiltrates (viral) or lobar consolidation (secondary bacterial).

---

## 🏥 Management

### 1. Antiviral Therapy (Neuraminidase Inhibitors)
* **First-line**: **Oseltamivir** (Tamiflu) **75 mg PO bid for 5 days**. (Alternatively: IV Peramivir or inhaled Zanamivir).
* **The 48-Hour Window Rule**: 
  * > [!IMPORTANT]
    > **Antiviral Timing**: Antivirals provide the greatest benefit (reducing illness duration by 1-2 days and decreasing complications) when initiated **within 48 hours of symptom onset**.
  * *Exception*: **Initiate Oseltamivir even after 48 hours** in patients who are hospitalized, have severe/progressive illness, or are at high risk of complications (age $>65$, pregnant, chronic lung/heart disease, immunosuppression).
* **Alternative**: **Baloxavir marboxil** (cap-dependent endonuclease inhibitor) as a single oral dose.

### 2. Supportive Care & Prevention
* Rest, aggressive oral hydration, and antipyretics (Acetaminophen. **Avoid Aspirin in children/adolescents** due to risk of **Reye Syndrome**).
* **Annual Vaccination**: Trivalent or quadrivalent inactivated influenza vaccine recommended for all individuals $\ge 6$ months of age, particularly high-risk groups and healthcare workers.

---

## 💡 Clinical Pearls

* > [!WARNING]
  > **Post-Flu Staph Pneumonia**: A patient recovering from influenza who suddenly develops worsening high fever, severe productive cough with blood-tinged sputum, and cavitary infiltrates on chest X-ray has secondary **Staphylococcal necrotizing pneumonia (MRSA)**. Add IV Vancomycin or Linezolid immediately.
* > [!TIP]
  > **Drift vs. Shift**:
  > * Antigenic **D**rift = **D**radual (Gradual) change $\rightarrow$ Epidemics (A & B).
  > * Antigenic **S**hift = **S**udden (Sudden) change $\rightarrow$ Pandemics (A only).
