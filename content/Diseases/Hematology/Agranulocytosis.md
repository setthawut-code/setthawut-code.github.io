# 🩸 Agranulocytosis (ภาวะเม็ดเลือดขาวต่ำรุนแรง)

> **Definition**: A severe, acute reduction in the number of circulating granulocytes (specifically neutrophils), defined as an Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) **$<500/\mu\text{L}$**, leading to an extreme susceptibility to life-threatening infections.

---

## 📊 Etiology & Offending Drugs
Most commonly drug-induced (idiosyncratic, non-dose-dependent immune-mediated destruction or direct myelotoxicity):
* **Antithyroid Drugs** (Classic cause): **Propylthiouracil (PTU)** and **Methimazole** (occurs in $0.2-0.5\%$ of patients, typically within the first 90 days of starting therapy).
* **Atypical Antipsychotics**: **Clozapine** (carries a $1\%$ risk; requires mandatory weekly ANC monitoring for the first 6 months).
* **Antibiotics**: **Co-trimoxazole** (TMP-SMX), Sulfonamides, Penicillins.
* **Antiepileptics**: Carbamazepine, Phenytoin.
* **NSAIDs**: Dipyrone (Metamizole), Phenylbutazone.
* **Chemotherapy**: Predictable, dose-dependent myelosuppression.

---

## 🔍 Clinical Manifestations
* **Neutropenic Fever**: Abrupt onset of high fever, chills, and shaking.
* **Necrotizing Ulcerative Lesions**: **Severe sore throat** (ulcerative pharyngitis), painful oral ulcers (stomatitis), and gingivitis.
* **Rapid-Onset Sepsis**: Pneumonia, urinary tract infections, or bacteremia progressing rapidly to septic shock.
* > [!IMPORTANT]
     > **Atypical Infection Presentation**: Due to the severe lack of neutrophils, patients with agranulocytosis cannot mount a normal inflammatory response. Classic signs of infection like **pus formation, localized redness, or fluctuance may be completely absent** despite severe tissue infection. Fever may be the only sign.

---

## 🔬 Investigations
* **CBC**: Total WBC count is low, with **ANC $<500/\mu\text{L}$**. Hemoglobin and platelet counts are characteristically **normal** (rules out [[Aplastic Anemia]] and leukemia).
  * *ANC calculation*: $\text{WBC count} \times \frac{\% \text{Neutrophils} + \% \text{Bands}}{100}$.
* **Bone Marrow Aspirate**: Shows a **selective absence/maturation arrest of the myeloid lineage** (at the promyelocyte stage), while erythroid and megakaryocytic lineages are intact.

---

## 🏥 Management

### 1. Stop the Offending Drug (First & Most Critical Step)
* Immediately discontinue the suspected drug (e.g., PTU, Methimazole, Clozapine). **This is non-negotiable.** Never re-challenge the patient with the same drug.

### 2. Neutropenic Fever Management
* > [!CAUTION]
  > **Neutropenic Fever Emergency**: If a patient with suspected or confirmed agranulocytosis presents with a temperature **$\ge 38.3^\circ\text{C}$** (or $\ge 38.0^\circ\text{C}$ sustained for $>1$ hour), draw blood cultures and **administer empiric anti-pseudomonal IV antibiotics within 1 hour** (e.g., **Piperacillin-Tazobactam 4.5 g IV q6h** OR **Cefepime 2 g IV q8h**). Do not wait for lab results or chest X-rays.
* Add Vancomycin if hemodynamic instability, line infection, or MRSA colonization is suspected.

### 3. Hematopoietic Growth Factors
* **Granulocyte Colony-Stimulating Factor (G-CSF)** (e.g., **Filgrastim 5 mcg/kg/day SC**): Speeds up myeloid recovery, shortening the duration of severe neutropenia and hospitalization.

---

## 💡 Clinical Pearls

* > [!IMPORTANT]
  > **PTU/Methimazole Warning Rule**: When initiating antithyroid therapy (PTU or Methimazole), **always counsel the patient to stop the medication immediately and go to the nearest emergency room for a CBC if they develop a fever, sore throat, or mouth ulcers.** Do not wait for a scheduled follow-up.
* > [!WARNING]
  > **The False Safety of Routine CBCs**: Idiosyncratic agranulocytosis occurs abruptly (within hours). Routine monthly CBC monitoring for PTU/Methimazole is not cost-effective and does not prevent agranulocytosis. Patient education on symptoms is the most effective safety measure.
* > [!TIP]
  > **Clozapine Monitoring**: Clozapine has a strict registry system. If ANC drops below $1,500/\mu\text{L}$, monitoring frequency increases. If ANC drops below **$<1,000/\mu\text{L}$**, Clozapine must be permanently discontinued.
